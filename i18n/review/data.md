# Works catalogue, speeches and Constitution data — points for a reviewer

## Possible errors in the English source data (check and fix in both languages)

- **works: The Cabinet Mission and the Untouchables.** The English dates the Cabinet Mission's statement "10 May 1946", but it is usually dated 16 May 1946.
- **constitution: article 394.** The English said he moved the motion "on 26 November 1949". **Fixed:** he moved it on 17 November 1949 and the Assembly adopted the Constitution on 26 November 1949.

## Translation choices

- **Glossary fix.** "What Congress and Gandhi Have Done **to** the Untouchables" is now "…अस्पृश्यांचे काय केले?". The earlier "…अस्पृश्यांसाठी" meant "for" and lost his irony.
- "The Untouchables (Who Were They…)" → "अस्पृश्य मूळचे कोण? ते अस्पृश्य का बनले?".
- "Imperial Finance" → साम्राज्य-वित्त, meaning the central government's finance, not "imperialist".
- "National Dividend" → राष्ट्रीय लाभांश (the old term for national income).
- "Communal Deadlock" → सांप्रदायिक तिढा, so it is not confused with caste.
- "the very soul of the Constitution and the very heart of it" → “संविधानाचा आत्माच आणि त्याचे हृदयच”.
- "a hack" → भाडोत्री तट्टू, used the same way everywhere.
