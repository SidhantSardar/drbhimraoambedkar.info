## Volume-wide consistency checklist

Settled across the volume while it was being translated:

- infallible / infallibility → अचूक / अचूकता (Appendix V's अमोघता changed to match); authority of scripture → प्रामाण्य.
- Ibid → तत्रैव everywhere (26 footnotes that read उपरोक्त, each checked to translate “Ibid”, were changed to match Volumes 1–3).
- creeds → मतप्रणाली (Riddle 11's मतपंथ changed); cults → उपासनापंथ.
- The title of Riddle 22 is quoted on p. 356 in the form its own chapter uses: ब्रह्म म्हणजे धर्म नव्हे.
- Readers see chapter titles from `library/toc-mr.json` (“2. कोडे क्र. 1 …”), not the translators' titles (“2. कोडे क्रमांक 1 — …”); the two differ only in wording.

For a reviewer to decide:

- **Plural or plain forms for ancient authors.** शंकराचार्य, जैमिनी, बादरायण and the sutra authors are plain in Riddles 1–9 and the Vedas appendices, but शंकराचार्य, पतंजली, पाणिनी take the respectful plural in Riddles 16–18 and “the Great Shankaracharya” in Riddle 22. Manu and the gods are plain everywhere; modern scholars plural everywhere.
- **Avatar table.** Riddle 10 (p. 83) keeps Jadna and Prithi in Latin; Appendix III (p. 179), the same table, writes यज्ञ and पृथू.
- **Angis** → अंगिस (Riddle 8) / अंगिर (Appendix I); probably Angiras, अंगिरस्.
- **“pundariketi vedanteshu nigadyate”** is in Devanagari in Riddle 8 and in Latin in Appendix I.
- **Srautmani** → सौत्रामणी (Riddle 20) / Latin (Riddle 24).
- **Brahma / Brahman.** Each translator used ब्रह्म where the Absolute is plainly meant and ब्रह्मा for the god; the oblique forms (ब्रह्माचा, ब्रह्मात) look the same for both, so a few places stay as ambiguous as the English.
- **Chapter 7's title** is cut to “moral or spiritual value?” in the contents data on both the English and Marathi sites (the contents builder misread the heading). It is Riddle No. 6, “The Contents of the Vedas: Have they any moral or spiritual value?”; the fix belongs in `scripts/build-toc.mjs`, keeping the slug.
