# Volume 5 — passages for a Marathi reviewer

The Marathi translation of these chapters was drafted with AI (Claude) following `i18n/marathi-style-guide.md`. The translators flagged the passages below because the English source is garbled by scanning, or because the meaning was hard to render. A reviewer should check each one against the printed PDF page and mark the chapter as reviewed.

Volume 5 is *Essays on Untouchables and Untouchability*. Translators were told to keep every bit of his anger and irony, to write अस्पृश्य for “Untouchables” (never दलित), to keep his “Mr. Gandhi” as श्री. गांधी, and to keep every case report, name, date and figure exactly as printed. A word supplied where the print has dropped or garbled a negative is shown in square brackets, e.g. [नाही]. Each paragraph that runs over a page break carries a `[[page N]]` mark in the source; the translators placed it at the matching point so every Marathi page holds what its PDF page holds.
