# Volume 3 — passages for a Marathi reviewer

The Marathi translation of these chapters was drafted with AI (Claude) following `i18n/marathi-style-guide.md`. The translators flagged the passages below because the English source is garbled by scanning, or because the meaning was hard to render. A reviewer should check each one against the printed PDF page and mark the chapter as reviewed.

Volume 3 holds his unpublished writings. Translators were told to keep his force against scripture and Brahminism, to add no honorifics he did not write (gods and epic figures take plain forms, the Buddha the respectful plural), and to translate the English rendering of each verse he quotes rather than a standard Marathi version. A word supplied where the print has dropped or garbled a negative is shown in square brackets, e.g. महापातक [नाही].
