# Volume 1 — passages for a Marathi reviewer

The Marathi translation of these chapters was drafted with AI (Claude) following `i18n/marathi-style-guide.md`. The translators flagged the passages below because the English source is garbled by scanning, or because the meaning was hard to render. A reviewer should check each one against the printed PDF page and mark the chapter as reviewed.

## Annihilation of Caste — Part 1 (PDF pp. 38–56)

1. **p. 38.** The heading "ANNIHILATION OF CASTE WITH" runs on to "A Reply to Mahatma Gandhi". It is rendered as the heading "जातिनिर्मूलन" plus "(महात्मा गांधींना दिलेल्या उत्तरासह)".
2. **p. 51.** The garbled Sanskrit "o.kkZuke czkã.kks xq#," is Kruti Dev font encoding. It was converted to "वर्णानाम् ब्राह्मणो गुरुः". **Check against the printed page.**
3. **p. 40.** He calls his 1917 essay "Castes in India, their Origin and their Mechanism". His wording is kept: “भारतातील जाती : त्यांची उत्पत्ती आणि त्यांची यंत्रणा”.
4. **p. 50.** "Rajgriha, Dadar, Bombay 14 15th May 1936" is read as postal district 14 and the date 15 May.
5. **p. 55.** "ghee (butter)" is rendered as "तूप". The gloss "(butter)" was dropped, because "लोणी" would be wrong.
6. **pp. 56–57.** A sentence runs across Parts 1 and 2. It is joined as "…प्रचलित होत्या आणि त्या / त्यांना स्वतःला व्यक्तिशः जाणवत होत्या."
7. **p. 42.** "Independent Harijans of Punjab" is unclear in the source and is kept literal: "पंजाबमधील स्वतंत्र हरिजन".
8. **p. 43.** The English "determined to wade through thick and thin but would not give up the idea of your presidentship" is awkward. Rendering: "कितीही संकटे आली तरी त्यांतून वाट काढण्याचा निर्धार करून बसले आहेत, पण आपल्या अध्यक्षपदाची कल्पना ते सोडायला तयार नाहीत".
9. **p. 48.** "I cared more for my faith than for any honour": "श्रद्धा" keeps the double sense of "faith".

## Annihilation of Caste — Part 2 (PDF pp. 57–75)

1. **p. 57.** "Mr. Redmond, the representative Ireland" has a missing word. It is rendered "आयर्लंडचे प्रतिनिधी श्री. रेडमंड", without guessing the word.
2. **p. 57.** "Home Rule in Ireland would be Rome Rule" is rendered "होमरूल म्हणजे ‘रोमरूल’ (रोमचे राज्य)". The pun is lost.
3. **p. 59.** "Commitia Centuriata" is a misprint for Comitia; it is kept in brackets. His statement that it was "an assembly of Plebians" is kept as written.
4. **p. 61.** The broken grammar of "whether he minds one class ill-treating… and thus allow tyranny…" is rendered following its evident sense.
5. **p. 63.** "distinctions of Castes or really distinctions of race" is read as "are really". Also: "ex hypothesia" is rendered (ex hypothesi), and "Prof. Batcson" as Bateson.
6. **p. 65.** "Hindus are a C 3 people" is kept as printed. "Demoralized" is rendered "नीतिभ्रष्ट" (morally corrupted); **check this choice.**
7. **p. 66.** In "the insult their ancestors gave to Shivaji", "their" is read as the Brahmins' ancestors.
8. **p. 70.** "make him off from" is read as "mark him off from". In the Morris quote, the misprints "reading down" and "heating down" are read as "treading" and "beating".
9. **p. 71.** Three readings:
   - "persecuting the reforms" is read as "reformers";
   - "New a caste" is read as "Now a caste";
   - "where mere is no slavery" is read as "there".
10. **p. 73.** In "those individuals also in whose favour there is birth…", "also" is kept literally.
11. **p. 74 — most important.** "The former is based on worth" follows "caste… Varna", so grammatically "former" points to caste, which contradicts his argument. The official text prints it this way, and it is translated literally: "यांपैकी पहिले योग्यतेवर आधारलेले आहे". **An editor should decide.**
12. **p. 75.** "incompitable with their startification" and "pigeon men into holes" are rendered by their evident sense.

## Annihilation of Caste — Part 4 (PDF pp. 95–113, end of the speech and the reply to Mahatma Gandhi)

1. **p. 102, footnote.** The Kruti Dev text is converted as "अंत्यजाचा विटाळ ज्यासी । गंगास्नाने शुद्धत्व त्यासी ॥ — एकनाथी भागवत". The chapter and ओवी numbers use unknown glyphs and are marked [मूळ मजकूर अस्पष्ट]. **Check against the printed page.**
2. **p. 97.** "eternal varieties" is read as "eternal verities" (शाश्वत सत्ये). "those who are by their learning and influence among the Savarnas" is broken English and is rendered by its evident sense.
3. **p. 98.** Three readings:
   - "Raja Rain Mohan Roy" is an OCR error for राजा राममोहन रॉय;
   - "by and of the Varna" is read as "by any of the Varnas";
   - the missing "be" in "so utterly devoid of merit" is supplied.
4. **p. 100.** "I have certainly meant when I have said…" is read as "I certainly meant it when I said…".
5. **p. 104.** "accept personal character to make a man" is read as "expect".
6. **p. 107.** "worth-while Caste" is a hyphenation slip for "worth — while Caste".
7. **p. 110.** The stray scan artefact "zz" is left out.
8. **Idioms rendered by meaning:**
   - "chalk is from cheese" → जमीन-अस्मानाचा फरक
   - "blue-blooded Sanatani" → अस्सल खानदानी सनातनी
   - "hedge" → तळ्यात-मळ्यात
   - "good Samaritan" → परोपकारी सज्जन
   - "prostitution of a noble profession" → …बाजार मांडणे
   - "pimp" → भडवा
   - "snout of a pig" → डुकराचे थोबाड
9. **p. 95.** "I have decided to change." is kept without adding "(धर्म)", because the English does not say it.
10. **Register.** Gandhi speaks in his own first person. "The Mahatma" takes respectful plural verbs, and Dr. Ambedkar speaks as "मी".

## Castes in India (PDF pp. 18–37)

1. **p. 37.** The stray scan artefact "zz" is left out.
2. **p. 30.** "…is derivative of their observance is neither strict nor complete" is read as "…is derivative, their observance is…".
3. **p. 29, footnote 2.** "pp. 2-33" is kept as printed; it may be 32–33.
4. **p. 22.** In Senart's definition, "bound together by common occupations" probably should read "usages". It is kept literally as "समान व्यवसायांनी".
5. **p. 24.** "Sagotras (of the same class)" is kept literally.
6. **p. 27.** Two passages are kept literally:
   - "by this endogamy morals are preserved" may be a misprint for "endogamy and morals";
   - "numerical disparity … is conveniently maintained" logically means parity.
7. **p. 35.** "varies directly with the distance" logically means inversely. It is kept literally as "समप्रमाणात".
8. **p. 26.** "all the three evils", though only two are named. It is kept literally.
9. **Numbers, not rights.** "equality between the sexes" and "sex disparity" refer to numbers. They are rendered "दोन्ही लिंगांमधील ही समानता" and "स्त्री-पुरुषांच्या संख्येतील विषमता" so readers do not take them as gender equality.
10. **Period terms kept.** "Negroes" → निग्रो and "Mohammedans" → मुसलमान are not modernised.
11. **pp. 30–32.** The sentence split "Preaching did not make / the caste system…" is joined across the page break.

## Annihilation of Caste — Part 3 (PDF pp. 76–94)

### Sanskrit stored in Kruti Dev font encoding (shown as Latin gibberish in the extracted text)

These were decoded to Devanagari. **Check each against the printed PDF page.**

1. **p. 86.** वर्णानाम् ब्राह्मणो गुरुः. The extract literally has "ब्राह्मण ।"; the standard reading is used.
2. **p. 86.** अनाम्नातेषु धर्मेषु कथं स्यादिति चेद्भवेत् । यं शिष्टा ब्राह्मणा ब्रूयुः स धर्मः स्यादशङ्कितः । (Manu 12.108)
3. **p. 87.** वेदः स्मृतिः सदाचारः स्वस्य च प्रियमात्मनः (Manu 2.12, first half only, as printed)
4. **p. 87.** योऽवमन्येत ते मूले हेतुशास्त्राश्रयात् द्विजः । स साधुभिर्बहिष्कार्यो नास्तिको वेदनिन्दकः ॥ (Manu 2.11)
5. **p. 87.** श्रुतिद्वैधं तु यत्र स्यात्तत्र धर्मावुभौ स्मृतौ । (Manu 2.14). The standard "स्यात्तत्र" is used.
6. **p. 87.** या वेदबाह्याः स्मृतयो याश्च काश्च कुदृष्टयः । सर्वास्ता निष्फलाः प्रेत्य तमोनिष्ठा हि ताः स्मृताः ॥ (Manu 12.95). Gaps are filled from the standard text.
7. **p. 87, Brihaspati.** वेदार्थोपनिबद्धत्वात् प्रामाण्यं हि मनोः स्मृतम् । मन्वर्थविपरीता तु या स्मृतिः सा न शस्यते ॥ **This is the least certain: the first word is badly garbled.**
8. **p. 87, Mahabharata.** पुराणं मानवो धर्मः सांगो वेदश्चिकित्सितम् । आज्ञासिद्धानि चत्वारि न हन्तव्यानि हेतुभिः ॥
9. **p. 89.** यद्यदाचर्यते येन धर्म्यं वाऽधर्म्यमेव वा । देशस्याचरणं नित्यं चरित्रं तद्धि कीर्तितम् ॥
10. **p. 89.** यस्मिन् देशे य आचारः पारंपर्यक्रमागतः । वर्णानां किल सर्वेषां स सदाचार उच्यते ॥ This is kept as printed, though it differs from the standard Manu 2.18.
11. **p. 89.** न देवचरितं चरेत्

### Garbled English, read by evident sense

- **p. 76.** "want only" → wantonly.
- **p. 78.** "in suffering" → suffrage, as in the 1936 original. "in famous" → infamous. "neither without you not with you" → nor.
- **p. 85.** "one bound to fight" → one is bound.
- **p. 87.** Three readings:
  - "an Ashta-dhikaras" → अष्टाधिकार;
  - "it is frozen, instantly…" → the sense that the caste freezes the moment it is told;
  - "supposed form or knowledge" → form of knowledge.
- **p. 91.** "as caused" → has caused.
- **p. 94.** "beneful" → baneful.
- **p. 84.** "third category": his own scheme names only two. It is kept as printed.

### Word choices worth a look

- "apply the dynamite to the Vedas" → सुरुंग लावावाच लागेल
- "herculean" → भगीरथ प्रयत्न
- "camp-followers" → बाजारबुणगे
- "wretched system" → दळभद्री व्यवस्था
- "Reason" → तर्कबुद्धी
- "Inter-marriage" → आंतरजातीय विवाह, so it is not confused with अंतर्विवाह (endogamy)
