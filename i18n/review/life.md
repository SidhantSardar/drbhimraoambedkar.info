# Life story and themes — points for a Marathi reviewer

These chapters were translated with AI (Claude) following `i18n/marathi-style-guide.md`. The translators flagged the choices below.

## Chapters 00–03

1. **00.** "*Gulamgiri* (Slavery)": the "(Slavery)" gloss is dropped because in Marathi it repeats the title. The gloss for Satyashodhak Samaj is kept.
2. **00.** "maid of all work" is rendered as the neutral idiom “हरकाम्या नोकर”, with the English in brackets.
3. **02.** "lived with an uncle in Bombay" (Ramabai) is rendered काका (father's side), but the English does not say which side. **Confirm काका or मामा.**
4. **01.** "Pendse sent his own son to take the soaked boy home": "home" is left as unclear as in the English. Keer suggests it was the teacher's home.
5. **02.** Bombay is बॉम्बे only at its first mention ("today's Mumbai") and मुंबई everywhere else:
   - Bombay Army → मुंबई सैन्य
   - Bombay Presidency → मुंबई इलाखा
   - Bombay Legislative Council → मुंबई कायदेमंडळ
6. **02.** "inflate the boy's head" is rendered as the idiom "डोक्यात हवा जाईल".
7. **00.** Phule's dedication is kept as a title-style phrase, as in the English.
8. **Name spellings to confirm:**

   - चां. भ. खैरमोडे
   - स. का. बोले
   - सयाजीराव गायकवाड तिसरे

## Chapters 08–10

1. **10, the 22 vows.** These are given in **his original Marathi words**, as administered at Deekshabhoomi, not translated back from English. The text was taken from Marathi Wikipedia's "बावीस प्रतिज्ञा". **Check every word against the printed sheet** on Wikimedia Commons ("22 Vows (in Marathi) administered … at Deeksha Bhoomi, Nagpur"). The official English in BAWS Vol. 17 Part 3 swaps vows 15 and 16, and the intro text says so.
2. **10.** The attribution "(translated from Marathi)" is rendered "(मूळ मराठी भाषणाच्या इंग्रजी अनुवादावरून)". The quote is a Marathi rendering of an English translation, not his own Marathi words. **If the original Marathi speech text can be found, use it instead.**
3. **08.** In the Krishnamachari quote, "engaged in State affairs" is rendered neutrally: government business or a princely State.
4. **08.** "a right is only a 'pious' wish" → “पवित्र” सदिच्छा. This is literal; the idiom means a wish with no force.
5. **08.** "a hack" → भाडोत्री लेखक.
6. **08 subtitle.** "chief defender" → प्रमुख बचावकर्ते. प्रमुख समर्थक reads more easily but loses the sense of defending the text in debate.
7. **09.** "balkanisation of the South" → "लहान-लहान तुकड्यांत विभाजन (balkanisation)".
8. **10.** "compilation and assembly plant" is kept as a literal metaphor.
