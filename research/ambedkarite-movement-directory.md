### Anna Mahizhnan

**Where:** Australia · **Kind of work:** Law & rights · **Status:** Active · **Quiet worker**

**What they do:** President of the Periyar Ambedkar Thoughts Circle of Australia (PATCA), which campaigns for caste discrimination to be recognised in Australian law.

Dr Anna Mahizhnan is president of the Periyar Ambedkar Thoughts Circle of Australia (PATCA) [source: The Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/india/australia-takes-historic-steps-to-address-caste-discrimination-national-anti-racism-framework-unveiled). PATCA is a group named after Periyar and Ambedkar that works against caste discrimination in Australia and has made submissions to federal and state governments [source: Inner West Council (NSW)](https://www.innerwest.nsw.gov.au/settlement-services-and-programs-inner-west/periyar-ambedkar-thoughts-circle-australia-patca). It says it sent more than 30 submissions to the Australian Human Rights Commission in 2022 and has asked New South Wales to make caste a protected attribute in law [source: PATCA](https://patca.org.au/about-us). When the Commission's National Anti-Racism Framework recognised caste discrimination in November 2024, she called it a major win for caste-oppressed communities and said PATCA was ready to help put it into practice [source: The Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/india/australia-takes-historic-steps-to-address-caste-discrimination-national-anti-racism-framework-unveiled).

### Dr. Ambedkar International Mission (AIM) Australia

**Where:** Melbourne and Adelaide, Australia · **Kind of work:** Grassroots organising · **Status:** Active · **Quiet worker**

**What they do:** Volunteer community group that teaches Ambedkar's ideas, promotes his Buddhism and campaigns for caste discrimination to be banned in Australian law.

AIM Australia is a not-for-profit community group, part of the worldwide Dr. Ambedkar International Mission network, whose work includes teaching Ambedkar's ideas, campaigning for equality and promoting Navayana (Ambedkar's form of Buddhism) [source: Dr. Ambedkar International Mission Australia](https://www.ambedkarmission.net/). In 2016, Dr Rupali Bhamare of Melbourne spoke for the group about Ambedkar's life when Western Sydney University's law school received a bust of him [source: Indian Link](https://indianlink.com.au/?p=58716). In March 2024 it held a free day of talks at Fed Square in Melbourne for Ambedkar's 133rd birth anniversary, with speakers from Australia and India [source: Fed Square](https://fedsquare.com/events/dr-ambedkars-133rd-birth-anniversary). Together with the Shri Guru Ravidass Sabha, it led the study 'Caste in Australia', funded by the Australian Human Rights Commission, in which 146 people from caste-oppressed communities were interviewed [source: University of Oxford, Faculty of Law](https://www.law.ox.ac.uk/content/news/asang-wankhede-leads-study-caste-discrimination-australia). The group says it is pushing for Australia's Racial Discrimination Act 1975 to name caste discrimination clearly [source: Dr. Ambedkar International Mission Australia](https://www.ambedkarmission.net/).

### Mitra Pariyar

**Where:** Australia and United Kingdom · **Kind of work:** Scholarship & archives · **Status:** Active · **Since:** 2008

**What they do:** Nepali Dalit anthropologist who studies caste among Nepali migrants abroad and argues in Nepal's press for Ambedkar's ideas.

Mitra Pariyar is a Nepali Dalit rights activist and anthropologist who studied at Oxford and has worked at universities in Australia and England (Dalit — people the caste system treated as 'untouchable') [source: The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/columns/2025/04/15/ambedkarism-with-a-nepali-flavour). He began reading Ambedkar seriously while doing an anthropology degree at Oxford in late 2008 [source: The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/columns/2025/04/15/ambedkarism-with-a-nepali-flavour). His research follows Nepali migrants abroad, including Gurkha soldiers' families settling in Britain and Nepali migrants in Australia [source: Macquarie University](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/publications/travelling-castes-nepalese-immigrants-in-australia/)[source: Macquarie University (Figshare)](https://figshare.mq.edu.au/articles/thesis/Overseas_caste_among_military_migrants_the_migration_and_settlement_of_Nepalese_Gurkhas_in_Britain/19430816). His 2019 study found that caste had gained new life overseas, helped along by the internet and smartphones [source: Macquarie University](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/publications/travelling-castes-nepalese-immigrants-in-australia/). In Nepal's newspapers he argues that Ambedkar's ideas offer Nepali Dalits their best hope for equality [source: The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/columns/2025/04/15/ambedkarism-with-a-nepali-flavour).

### Vikrant Kishore

**Where:** Australia (teaches in Ningbo, China) · **Kind of work:** Music, film & arts · **Status:** Active · **Quiet worker**

**What they do:** Australian filmmaker and academic whose documentary Resisting Casteism in Australia records Dalit experiences of caste in the diaspora.

Vikrant Kishore is an Australian filmmaker, journalist and academic who teaches as an associate professor at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China [source: The Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/discussion-interview/fighting-caste-discrimination-a-conversation-with-documentary-film-maker-vikrant-kishore)[source: The Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/amp/story/society/resisting-casteism-in-australia-to-premiere-at-melbourne-documentary-film-festival-2026). His documentary Resisting Casteism in Australia follows him for about 3,500 km through Melbourne, Sydney, Newcastle and Coffs Harbour to hear Dalit voices, and it was set to premiere at the Melbourne Documentary Film Festival in July 2026 [source: The Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/amp/story/society/resisting-casteism-in-australia-to-premiere-at-melbourne-documentary-film-festival-2026). He has spoken publicly about the caste questions he has faced in Australia, including an incident in 2006 while he was teaching there [source: The Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/dalit-news/dalit-history-month-caste-discrimination-register-how-will-it-help-dalits-fight-casteism-in-australia)[source: Radio 4EB (The Wire)](https://www.4eb.org.au/news/casteism-recognised-as-racial-discrimination-in-australia-but-how-to-fight-it-2/). He also offers free advice to Bahujans (people from communities kept low by caste) who plan to study or work in Australia [source: The Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/discussion-interview/fighting-caste-discrimination-a-conversation-with-documentary-film-maker-vikrant-kishore).

### Anne Murphy

**Where:** Vancouver, Canada · **Kind of work:** Scholarship & archives · **Status:** Active · **Since:** 2020

**What they do:** Canadian scholar who leads the Caste in Canada project, recording the stories of Ambedkarite and anti-caste community members.

Anne Murphy is a scholar at the University of British Columbia whose research covers Punjabi language and history in British Columbia, memories of the 1947 Partition and the Komagata Maru ship incident [source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/?p=2698). From 2020 to 2023 she led a project called Caste in Canada, which profiles and interviews community members, followed by a second phase called The Elimination of Caste from 2024 to 2026 [source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/?p=2698). The project features activists such as Jai Birdi and the late writer Mohan Lal Karimpuri, who spread Ambedkar's teachings through poetry and activism after moving to Canada in 2006 [source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/?p=2698)[source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy, Caste in Canada project)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/research/caste-in-canada/mohan-lal/). It also funds a postdoctoral fellowship in Dalit studies for 2024 to 2026, helping train new researchers (Dalit — people the caste system treated as 'untouchable') [source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/?p=2698). Its recorded interviews create a public record of how Ambedkarites have organised in Canada [source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/?p=2698)[source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy, Caste in Canada project)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/research/caste-in-canada/mohan-lal/).

### Jai Birdi

**Where:** British Columbia, Canada · **Kind of work:** Grassroots organising · **Status:** Active · **Quiet worker**

**What they do:** Social services manager who leads the Chetna Association of Canada, winning Ambedkar memorials, lectures and city proclamations in British Columbia.

Jai Birdi came to Canada with his family in the mid-1970s, settled on the west coast in the early 1980s and built a career in social services, rising to senior management [source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy, Caste in Canada project)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/research/caste-in-canada/jai-birdi/). He says his interest in caste barriers grew from curiosity rather than from facing caste oppression himself, and he led the volunteer efforts that became the Chetna Association of Canada, where he is executive director [source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy, Caste in Canada project)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/research/caste-in-canada/jai-birdi/). He helped place memorials to Ambedkar, including a bust at Simon Fraser University, started a Dr. Ambedkar memorial lecture series and persuaded local governments to issue proclamations honouring Ambedkar [source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy, Caste in Canada project)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/research/caste-in-canada/jai-birdi/). In May 2024 he helped steer a gathering of more than 200 delegates on Parliament Hill in Ottawa, where MPs made statements about Ambedkar in the House of Commons [source: The Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/india/in-a-first-jai-bhim-resounds-in-canadian-parliament-push-for-nobel-prize-recognition-for-baba-saheb-ambedkar). He hopes to see caste added as a protected category in Canadian law and to build a centre guided by Ambedkar's philosophy [source: University of British Columbia (Anne Murphy, Caste in Canada project)](https://blogs.ubc.ca/annemurphy/research/caste-in-canada/jai-birdi/).

### Christophe Jaffrelot

**Where:** Paris, France and London, United Kingdom · **Kind of work:** Scholarship & archives · **Status:** Active · **Since:** 2000

**What they do:** French political scientist whose book on Ambedkar and untouchability reached readers from India to Hungary.

Christophe Jaffrelot is a French political scientist who studies caste and democracy in India and has taught at Sciences Po in Paris and at King's College London's India Institute [source: Hurst Publishers](https://www.hurstpublishers.com/book/ambedkar-in-london/)[source: Sciences Po Alumni](https://sciencespo-alumni.fr/fr/event/discussion-on-a-critical-engagement-with-dr-ambedkar-s-ideas-legacy-with-prof-christophe-jaffrelot/2024/11/13/7822). Hurst published his book Dr Ambedkar and Untouchability in 2005, which looks at Ambedkar as a thinker about society, as a statesman and as a critic of caste who turned to Buddhism [source: Hurst Publishers](https://www.hurstpublishers.com/book/dr-ambedkar-and-untouchability/). His French book on Ambedkar and India's Dalits inspired the Hungarian sociologist Tibor Derdák, who saw parallels with the Roma and went on to help found Europe's Ambedkar school [source: Homegrown](https://homegrown.co.in/homegrown-voices/why-is-a-high-school-in-hungary-named-after-dr-ambedkar). In 2022 he co-edited Ambedkar in London with William Gould and Santosh Dass [source: Hurst Publishers](https://www.hurstpublishers.com/book/ambedkar-in-london/). He continues to speak publicly about Ambedkar's ideas, including at a Sciences Po discussion in 2024 [source: Sciences Po Alumni](https://sciencespo-alumni.fr/fr/event/discussion-on-a-critical-engagement-with-dr-ambedkar-s-ideas-legacy-with-prof-christophe-jaffrelot/2024/11/13/7822).

### Maren Bellwinkel-Schempp

**Where:** Heidelberg, Germany · **Kind of work:** Scholarship & archives · **Status:** Died 2011 · **Since:** 1972 · **Quiet worker**

**What they do:** German anthropologist who spent decades studying Dalit life and Ambedkarite Buddhism in Kanpur and wrote on Ambedkar studies in Germany.

Maren Bellwinkel-Schempp was a German social anthropologist trained at Heidelberg University's South Asia Institute, whose research and solidarity work were devoted to India's Dalits (people the caste system treated as 'untouchable') [source: Südasien 3/2011](https://www.maren-bellwinkel.de/artikel/nachruf.pdf). From 1972 she did fieldwork among Dalits in the industrial city of Kanpur [source: Südasien 3/2011](https://www.maren-bellwinkel.de/artikel/nachruf.pdf). In a 2007 article titled 'From Bhakti to Buddhism: Ravidas and Ambedkar', she traced how Kanpur's Dalits came to honour the saint Ravidas and then, in the 1980s, accepted Buddhism, which took shape there through a temple, a monk, a lay committee and its own rites [source: Economic and Political Weekly (2007), via Digital Library of Buddhist Studies](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/en/search/search_detail.jsp?seq=684429). In a 2003 report she described Ambedkar studies at Heidelberg and noted that Ambedkar was gaining recognition in German academic and political circles [source: SAI Report, Summer 2003](https://www.maren-bellwinkel.de/artikel/ambedkar.pdf). She died in Stuttgart in July 2011 [source: Südasien 3/2011](https://www.maren-bellwinkel.de/artikel/nachruf.pdf).

### János Orsós (Orsós János)
