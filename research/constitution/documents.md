## How the BAWS extracts were made

```
.venv/bin/python -I -c "from pypdf import PdfReader, PdfWriter; ..."   # copy page objects i..j unchanged, add /Title and /Subject metadata
```

Page ranges (1-based PDF pages of `Volume13.pdf`): Draft 128–350; speech of 4 Nov 1948 82–103; Article 25 debate 467–476; closing speech and adoption 1239–1252. For most of BAWS Vol. 13, printed page = PDF page − 33.
