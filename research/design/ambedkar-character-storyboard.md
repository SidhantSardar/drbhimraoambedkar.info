# Dr. B. R. Ambedkar — illustrated character storyboard

## Art direction

Create a dignified, hand-painted vintage editorial-cartoon treatment rather than a caricature.  The character should read as the same person at every age: a broad, thoughtful oval face; a high forehead; a calm, direct gaze; neatly parted dark hair in youth, progressively receding and closely cropped in later life; and spectacles that become an enduring silhouette. Use warm, subdued Indian print colours—indigo, umber, cream, maroon and muted saffron—with visible paper grain, restrained ink outlines and no photorealism.  Clothing must be era-appropriate. Do not add captions, slogans, flags, halos, watermarking, or political party symbols.

## Eight-panel storyboard

Use a consistent 16:9 landscape panel, with the main character roughly 55% of panel height. The years are factual anchors; background details are evocative illustrations, not claims that a particular photograph exists.

1. **1891–1900 · Mhow, Dapoli and Satara — the young Bhiva**
   - Young boy walking with a cloth-wrapped book beside a modest cantonment-town street; warm dawn light.
   - Avoid adult spectacles and adult facial proportions.

2. **1900–1904 · A schoolboy’s determination**
   - A nine- to twelve-year-old Bhimrao studies at a small wooden desk, a gunny cloth folded beside him and a schoolbook open; keep the image quiet and humane, never melodramatic.

3. **1913–1916 · Student at Columbia**
   - A confident young Ambedkar in a dark Edwardian suit and round glasses walks across a scholarly campus carrying books; autumn leaves and stone architecture in the distance.

4. **1916–1923 · London and the barrister**
   - Young barrister at a desk with legal papers and a library shelf, wearing a restrained three-piece suit and round glasses; a soft lamp creates an intimate study scene.

5. **1924–1935 · Writer, organiser and advocate**
   - Mature Ambedkar addresses a small public gathering from a simple platform, holding notes; suit, glasses, intent gaze. The crowd is suggested, not individually detailed.

6. **1946–1950 · Drafting India’s Constitution**
   - Middle-aged Ambedkar seated at a large desk with a manuscript, fountain pen and stacks of papers; late-1940s suit, striped tie, angular glasses. Use a restrained government-hall interior.

7. **1950–1956 · Scholar and Buddhist leader**
   - Older Ambedkar outdoors with a book and a subtle Buddhist architectural silhouette at a distance; pale Nehru jacket, angular glasses, composed expression. No divine imagery.

8. **1956 · Final work at Rajgruha**
   - An elderly Ambedkar writing at a book-lined desk near a window, pale jacket and glasses, manuscripts carefully arranged. Show intellectual concentration and calm—not illness or distress.
